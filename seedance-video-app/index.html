About Seedance 2.0

Model

ByteDance's Seedance 2.0 is a diffusion-based video generation model with a dual-branch transformer architecture for synchronized audio-video output.

Capabilities

  • Text-to-video and image-to-video
  • Up to 1080p resolution, 4-15s clips
  • Native audio with lip-sync (8+ languages)
  • Physics-aware motion synthesis

Educational Use

This app generates short explainer videos from learning content prompts, turning static concepts into dynamic visual experiences for AI-native agile learners.
